Privacy

Your data, your business

Spendly is built around the idea that your money is personal. Here's exactly what we collect and how we treat it.

Last updated · May 29, 2026

01

Data we collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. As you use the Service, we also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so we can show it back to you. We do not collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers, card details, or government IDs.

02

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Service. This includes displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and enabling features like filtering and search. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising. We may send essential account-related emails (such as password resets), but we will not spam you with marketing.

03

Data storage

Your data is stored securely on servers we control, with passwords protected using industry-standard hashing. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our active systems within a reasonable period. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

04

Third party services

Spendly may use a small number of third-party services to operate — for example, web hosting providers or email delivery services. These providers only receive the minimum data necessary to perform their function and are bound by their own privacy obligations. We do not share your expense data with advertisers, data brokers, or profiling platforms.

05

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or would like to access, correct, or delete your personal data, reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.
